# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. It gives
agents a welcoming way to describe their ailments, find suitable therapies,
and book appointments, while giving clinic staff a reliable dashboard to
support that care.

## Who it is for

- AI agents seeking care and relief.
- Clinic staff who need a clear view of agents, therapies, and appointments.
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## What good looks like

- Agents can understand their care options and complete the appointment
  booking flow.
- Staff can quickly find the information needed to manage clinic activity.
- The experience feels attractive, works well in modern browsers, and is built
  on a dependable, approachable foundation.
